ABOUT THE STUDIO

Engineering with purpose

FORGEYI is a digital product studio for people who run complex operations. We start with the decisions engineers make every day, from setting a production target to finding the workstation that holds up a line.

Built around the work

We look at how operators, engineers, and planners share information. Those handoffs guide what we build and where human judgment belongs.

Designed to grow

Manufacturing is our starting point. Our aim is to build software that can adapt as teams add processes, connect systems, and bring AI into their work.
